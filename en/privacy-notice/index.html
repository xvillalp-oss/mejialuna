Privacy Notice

Last updated November 18, 2026

Who is responsible for your personal data?

Mejía Luna, S.C., located at Av. Cordillera de los Andes 210, PH2, Lomas de Chapultepec III Sección, Miguel Hidalgo, 11000 Mexico City, Mexico, is responsible for processing your personal data. This notice applies when you visit our website, download our materials, request a consultation, or retain us, whether for yourself or on behalf of a business.

For privacy inquiries, email privacidad@mejialuna.mx.

What data do we use, and why?

  • If you request materials or schedule a consultation, we use the contact details you provide, such as your name, email address, and phone number, to deliver the materials, respond to you, or schedule your appointment. If you describe your situation, we use that information to assess your inquiry.
  • If you retain us, we use the contact, financial, banking, corporate, and operational information we need to advise you, develop a strategy, prepare filings, represent you, manage your case, and handle fees, invoices, and payments. Company records may contain personal data, such as the name and title of a representative or information about someone involved in a transaction.

We require your express consent to process personal data about your finances or assets unless an exception under Mexican law applies.

Advertising and contact lists shared with Meta and Google

We may also use your contact details for the optional purpose of sending you advertising by email and WhatsApp. If you authorize us to use your information for personalized ads, we include your email address or phone number in contact lists we upload to Meta and Google. These platforms match the identifiers to their users to show them our ads. They may process those identifiers outside Mexico.

You can accept or decline the sharing of your contact details with Meta and Google for this purpose. You can also choose whether to receive marketing messages directly from us. Your choices do not affect your ability to receive materials, consult with us, or retain us.

To tell us your preferences, withdraw your authorization, or opt out, email privacidad@mejialuna.mx. Include the email address or phone number you want added or removed and specify whether your request applies to email, WhatsApp, advertising lists, or all three. You can do this when you first contact us or at any time afterward.

Who has access to information about your case?

Members of the firm working on your case use the information they need to do their work. We use Microsoft Outlook for email and OneDrive to store and share documents within the team.

We may submit data and documents to courts with jurisdiction over your case when necessary to handle the matter, exercise or defend a right, or comply with a court order. When an exception under Mexican law applies, we may make the disclosure without additional authorization.

When you visit our website or request materials

We use ClickFunnels to receive requests for digital materials, Calendly to schedule appointments, and Google Maps to show our office location. When you load a page or use one of these services, the providers may receive your IP address, browser type, and information about your interactions. They may use cookies to operate their services. You can block or delete cookies in your browser’s privacy settings. If you prefer to schedule by phone, call +52 56 4852 7428.

How long do we keep personal data?

The retention period depends on why we use the data. For case files, we consider the duration of the matter, applicable legal obligations, and the time limits for addressing potential liability. Once those periods end, the data must be deleted, with access restricted beforehand where required.

How to exercise your privacy rights

Under Mexican law, you can ask what personal data we hold about you (access), ask us to correct errors (rectification), request deletion where appropriate (cancellation), or object to a particular use (opposition). These rights are known in Mexico as ARCO rights. You or your representative can exercise them by emailing privacidad@mejialuna.mx.

Include your name, a way to reach you, and what you are requesting. If you seek correction, deletion, or an end to a particular use, identify the data involved. For corrections, specify the change and provide supporting documentation. You may include a reference to help us locate the data. You must verify your identity. If you act for someone else, you must also show that you are authorized to represent them.

We will inform you of our decision within 20 business days after receiving your request. If we grant it, we will act on it within 15 business days after we notify you. When circumstances warrant, each deadline may be extended once for the same length of time. For access requests, we may provide an electronic copy once we verify your identity.

You can also use this email address to withdraw consent or limit the use or disclosure of your data. Tell us which use you want stopped and how we can reach you. Withdrawal applies going forward. If we are legally required to retain certain data, we will explain which parts of your request we can fulfill.

Changes to this notice

We will post the current version on this page and include the date it was updated. If a new purpose requires your consent, we will ask for it before using your data for that purpose.
