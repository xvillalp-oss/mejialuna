Rodrigo Mejía Luna

Founding Partner

Rodrigo graduated from Tecnológico de Monterrey’s Santa Fe campus. He has more than seven years of litigation experience, with a focus on commercial disputes. His training includes diploma programs in oral commercial proceedings, trial advocacy, and international commercial arbitration, as well as postgraduate studies in criminal procedure at Mexico’s National Institute of Criminal Sciences (INACIPE).

Fernando Olvera Vargas

Commercial Litigation Attorney

Fernando has more than ten years of experience leading litigation strategy and resolving complex disputes in law firms and in-house. His practice spans commercial, constitutional, and civil law, including high-stakes litigation, amparo proceedings, and challenges to administrative decisions. He handles disputes involving companies, financial transactions, trusts, and employment. He also drafts, negotiates, and manages civil and commercial contracts, partnership agreements, and corporate arrangements to help clients address risks before a dispute reaches court.
