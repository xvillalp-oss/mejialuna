Who We Are

A firm built to resolve disputes

Founded in Mexico City in 2023, Mejía Luna focuses on commercial and criminal litigation. We represent companies of all sizes, business owners, and individuals in complex, high-value disputes throughout Mexico.

We negotiate on our clients’ behalf and represent them in Mexico’s federal and local courts and before public prosecutors.

Our commercial and criminal litigation attorneys work together, so a client facing both types of proceedings can turn to one team.

Why choose Mejía Luna?
Two areas of focus. One firm.

Coordinated strategy When a case raises both commercial and criminal issues, we develop a strategy that addresses both.

Direct communication You stay in direct contact with the attorney handling your case.

Nationwide representation We appear in federal and local courts throughout Mexico.
