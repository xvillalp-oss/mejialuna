Mejía Luna, S.C. is a Mexico City law firm focused on commercial and criminal litigation. We represent businesses of all sizes and individuals throughout Mexico.

Some commercial disputes also raise criminal issues. Our attorneys handle both, so we can weigh litigation, negotiation, and practical business solutions together.
