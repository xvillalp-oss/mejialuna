The Role of the Law

The Role of the Law

Litigation can help people with limited resources protect their rights. Everyone deserves access to good legal representation, whatever their ability to pay.

We set aside part of our practice to advise, support, and represent people in vulnerable circumstances who are facing civil or family disputes. We provide these services free of charge.

How does it work?

We review each person’s circumstances individually and decide which matters we can take on. Our pro bono clients receive the same care and preparation we bring to every other case.

If you or someone you know is facing a difficult situation, contact us. We will review the case at no cost and with no obligation.
