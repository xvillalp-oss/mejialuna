Practice Areas · Criminal Litigation

Criminal Litigation

Our Mexico City criminal litigation team defends people accused of crimes and represents victims of property and financial offenses.

Practice Areas

Criminal practice areas

Criminal Defense

We defend individuals and companies accused of property and financial crimes. We review the investigation file, develop a defense, and appear at hearings throughout the case. Where appropriate, we seek dismissal, conditional suspension of proceedings, or a reparation agreement. We practice in federal and local courts throughout Mexico.

Representing Crime Victims

We represent businesses and individuals harmed by property and financial crimes. We help clients file a criminal complaint with the public prosecutor, submit evidence for the investigation, participate in the proceedings alongside the prosecution, and seek compensation for their losses.

Our Approach

How we work

We begin by assessing the client’s position. When a criminal case raises issues in other areas, we coordinate the defense or the victim’s representation with commercial or civil proceedings and any related amparo claims.

The attorney handling your case stays in direct contact with you and explains each stage of the proceedings in plain language.
