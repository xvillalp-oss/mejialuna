Practice Areas · Commercial Litigation

Commercial Litigation

We help businesses and individuals collect debts both in and out of court. We also defend clients against claims and lawsuits and represent them throughout commercial proceedings.

Our Practice

A litigation strategy tailored to the dispute

We represent businesses of all sizes and individuals in commercial disputes before local and federal courts in Mexico City and across Mexico. We identify the steps most likely to achieve the client’s objective and move promptly to put them into effect.

Practice Areas

Commercial Litigation Services

01

Commercial Debt Enforcement Proceedings

We help creditors collect debts backed by enforceable instruments such as promissory notes. In these proceedings, we can seek attachment of the debtor’s assets at the outset. We also defend debtors at every stage of a commercial enforcement action.

02

Oral Commercial Proceedings

We bring claims for nonpayment and breach of contract, including unpaid invoices and insurance claims. We build the case from the records our clients have, such as bank transfer confirmations, invoices, emails, quotes, and messages.

03

Prejudgment Attachment

When assets may be at risk before a lawsuit begins, we seek a court order to secure them. Depending on the case, the order may cover bank accounts, real estate, trademarks, shares and other equity interests, inventory, or receivables.

04

Pre-Suit Proceedings

Before filing the main action, we can ask a court to obtain admissions, order the production of documents, conduct inspections, or preserve evidence.

05

Amparo Relief in Commercial Cases

When a commercial court ruling affects a client’s fundamental rights, we pursue relief through Mexico’s constitutional review procedure, known as amparo. Our attorneys have extensive experience with both direct and indirect amparo proceedings.

06

Enforcement of Judgments

We continue to represent clients after judgment until they collect. We plan for the steps enforcement may require, including a judicial sale, a court-ordered transfer of assets, or the appointment of a receiver.

Our Approach

How we work

We begin by reviewing the facts, the evidence, and the legal merits of the case. We then compare litigation and negotiation, including the likely timeline, costs, and potential benefits. Before filing, we give you a clear plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Commercial Litigation FAQs

A commercial enforcement action (juicio ejecutivo mercantil) allows a creditor to collect a commercial debt based on an enforceable document, such as a promissory note or a judgment. One feature of this procedure is that the court can attach the debtor’s assets at the outset.

The main stages are filing the claim and seeking attachment, a formal demand for payment, the defendant’s answer, the presentation of evidence, closing arguments, judgment, and enforcement.

Prejudgment attachment is a court order that secures a debtor’s assets. It may be available before the main action begins when there is a substantiated risk that the debtor will conceal or transfer assets.
